## FACEBOOK

**California Dive Report — Week of July 27 – August 2, 2026**

Two forces ran the week, pulling opposite directions. La Jolla's real-time clarity came back online — the Scripps Pier turbidity sensor, dark since July 15, was **restored on 7/30** — and the water put on a show, clearing dramatically on the afternoon incoming tide (hazy 10–14 ft mornings opening to 25–30 ft by afternoon). Meanwhile a swell that peaked early in the week faded steadily toward the weekend, and it was the reason the south stayed uneven: surf magnified in the shallow reef coves blew out the exposed pockets while the deeper and offshore water ran clear. Monterey Bay stratified hard — a murky bloom on top, but the clearest water of the week sat below its thermocline. Catalina was flat and blue. Warm surface everywhere over cool, clean water below.

**🌊 SURF & SWELL — the fading driver**
The early-week swell was the main limiter, and it worked the shallow, SW-facing reef coves hardest — the reef and rock structure *magnifies* the surf, so those pockets churned out while the deeper water stayed clean:
- **Laguna took the brunt.** Crescent Bay ran a rough 8 ft on 7/31 (garibaldi looking like "dim orange lights in fog"), then dropped to ~5 ft on 8/2 as sets built to 4–5 ft — beach divers broadly called the OC coast 2–5 ft and stayed out.
- **Goff / Treasure Island** ran 15–20 ft on the calm 8/1 morning; when the swell's last push brought big waves, it still opened to 10–15 ft once you dropped below the surge.
- **La Jolla's shallows** stayed choppier than the reefs early/mid-week before the surf settled to an easy 2–3 ft by the weekend.
- **Net:** deeper, sheltered, and offshore water fished best; the surf-worked shallow coves held the murk. Pick your spot by exposure, not just the number. (The wider SoCal reports backed this up — Small Craft Advisories ran on the outer waters all week, 7–11 ft seas, easing only Tuesday.)

**🟢 SAN DIEGO / LA JOLLA — the sensor's back, and the tide runs the clarity**
- **The Scripps Pier turbidity sensor is back online (7/30)** after two weeks dark — real-time La Jolla clarity is restored.
- Strong **intraday clearing on the incoming tide**: 7/29 and 7/30 both ran hazy 10–14 ft in the morning and opened to a cam-confirmed **25–30 ft by afternoon**.
- **Cove & Marine Room hit 25–30 ft** on the calm, minimal-surge morning of 7/31 — the Cove even cleared to crystal inside the pocket by 8/2 afternoon (it cleans the longer you stay).
- Classic La Jolla stratification held all week — **clear up top, hazier below ~50 ft** (the exact opposite of Monterey). The Vallecitos shallows, murky for weeks, finally cleared.

**🟡 ORANGE COUNTY / LAGUNA**
Surf-worked (see above). The bright spot: **Corona del Mar / Pirates Cove held 15–20 ft all day on 8/2.**

**🔵 CATALINA — the gem**
**50–70 ft and no waves (8/1).** The clearest, calmest water of the week — and a stable mixed-bag fishery to match.

**🟢 MONTEREY BAY / CENTRAL COAST — stratified, gin-clear deep**
A persistent surface bloom capped genuinely spectacular water below the thermocline:
- Breakwater's "tale of two cities" (7/28): ~15 ft in the shallow kelp, a particulate 7–15 ft along the wall to 45 ft; 60°F surface / 53°F at depth.
- A boat dive off "Molla Mountain" (7/30): green-brown murk the first 20 ft, thermocline at 25, then **35+ ft below 40, opening to 40–60 ft by 60 ft down.**
- McAbee → Aquarium Intake → Metridium (8/1): pitch-black and low-vis under the kelp up top, but **30 ft+ clear below the thermocline** (58→50°F).
- South Monastery (8/1): sporty, a live bloom on top, but **30'+ at depth**, dark and beautiful.
- Below the thermocline, Monterey was the clearest water in California this week.

**🐟 FISH & OFFSHORE INTEL**
Warm, blue water offshore drove the fish story. A **yellowfin push crossed the border and ran north** — mostly small grade (5–15 lb) with a sample of 20–50s — showing within ~3 miles of Oceanside and up into LA/OC waters, plus the Coronado Canyon. **Bluefin (20 to 200+ lb)** held the cooler 68–73°F band out west (the 711 / 499 / Snail / west of Catalina), while yellowfin and **striped marlin — the best run in years —** worked the warmer 74–75°F side of the break around Catalina and south of Church Rock. Dorado scattered on the kelp paddies, and a **wahoo — a tropical, rare-for-here catch — turned up in the warm water down south off the Banda Bank.** Inshore, the warm water lit up the local game — the **best barracuda bite in years off PV / LA / Long Beach**, wide-open **bonito**, and mixed **yellowtail with good calico and sand bass**. Two warm-water tells: the **squid vanished** as temps climbed into the 70s, and **live bait got scarce** all week.

**🌡️ Heatwave:** SoCal surface 74–76°F; Monterey surface 55–64°F; at depth statewide a cool, clean 50–53°F. Sharp thermoclines everywhere — the good water is *down*.

**🌕 Moon:** Full moon midweek — now waning gibbous.

**WILDLIFE:** Green sea turtles back at La Jolla; a **50+ school of yellowtail (some pushing 4 ft) plus a curious school of triggerfish at the Marine Room**; a first-ever wild octopus at Corona del Mar; a sleeping angel shark, ratfish, and a huge baitfish school at South Monastery; a bay ray and a pigeon guillemot at McAbee/Metridium; leopard sharks, bat rays, and diamond rays over the La Jolla flats; sand dollars carpeting the sand past 60 ft in Monterey.
