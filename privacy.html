SpearFactor — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-08-28

SpearFactor provides ocean visibility and dive-condition forecasts for California, through our website (conditions.spearfactor.com) and our iOS and Android apps. This policy explains what we collect and why. The short version: we collect as little as possible, we don't sell your data, and most features work without any personal information at all.

Information we collect

  • Dive reports you choose to submit — the spot, visibility, water temperature, and any notes. These are shared publicly in the community "Recent Reports" feed. Don't include anything in a report you wouldn't want public.
  • Email address (optional) — only if you provide it to join our dive-report email list or attach it to a report.
  • Push notification token (app only) — if you allow notifications, your device's push token is stored so we can send visibility and swell alerts. It is not tied to your name or email.
  • Approximate location (optional, app/site) — only if you use a "nearest spot" feature and grant permission. It is used to pick a dive spot and is not stored on our servers.
  • Basic diagnostics — standard, aggregated request logs (e.g., which spot pages are loaded) used to keep the service running and improve forecasts. These are not used to identify you.

How we use it

  • To generate and display visibility forecasts and community reports.
  • To send visibility/swell alerts, if you opt in (app).
  • To send the dive-report email, if you subscribe (you can unsubscribe anytime).
  • To calibrate and improve the accuracy of our predictions.

Service providers we use

We rely on a few processors to run the service. They handle data only to provide their function, and we do not sell your information to anyone.

  • Cloudflare — website hosting, API, and storage.
  • Anthropic — powers an automated visibility estimate from a public underwater camera frame. No personal data is sent.
  • Apple & Google — deliver push notifications to your device.
  • Our email provider — delivers the dive-report email to subscribers.

Data retention

  • Submitted dive reports are retained to power the public feed and improve forecasts.
  • Push tokens automatically expire if unused, and are removed if you disable notifications.
  • Email addresses are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your choices

  • Use the forecasts without submitting any personal information.
  • Turn push notifications on or off in your device settings.
  • Deny or revoke location permission at any time.
  • Unsubscribe from the email at any time via the link in any email.
  • Request removal of a report or your email by contacting us (below).

Children

SpearFactor is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy; we'll revise the effective date above when we do.
